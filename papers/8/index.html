How strange: Phase diagrams with 3 critical points

Mateus Reinke Pelicer, Nikolas Cruz Camacho, Rajesh Kumar, Veronica Dexheimer, Jacquelyn Noronha-Hostler

We show that the Chiral Mean-Field model (CMF) can produce a phase diagram with three critical points: the nuclear liquid-gas transition, quark deconfinement, and a strangeness driven transition. The strangeness driven transition separates a mainly nucleonic phase from one dominated by hyperons and baryon resonances. We discuss the compositional change in these transitions and their possible signatures in heavy-ion collisions and neutron star mergers.
