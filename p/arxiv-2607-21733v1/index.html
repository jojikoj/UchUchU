JWST/MIRI Reveals the Evolution from Molecular to Atomic Disk Winds

Naman S. Bajaj, Ilaria Pascucci, Sylvie Cabrit, Suzan Edwards, Gabriele Cugno, Andrew D. Sellek, Joan R. Najita, Ke Zhang et al.

概要

The evolution and dispersal of protoplanetary disks--governed by accretion, magnetically launched jets and winds, and photoevaporative winds--fundamentally shape planetary systems. Determining how these mass-loss processes co-evolve is crucial for constraining planet formation pathways. We analyze archival JWST/MIRI/IFU data of 72 inclined (i>40deg) mostly ClassII disks to identify and characterize spatially resolved jets and winds, focusing on [NeII] and H2 lines.
