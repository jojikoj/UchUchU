SN2025aico: An Interesting Case Of $^{56}$Ni Mixing, Ejecta Asymmetries, and Dust Formation in a Type IIb Supernova

Grace Davis, Kyle Medler, Chris Ashall, Willem B. Hoogendam, Cameron Pfeffer, David O. Jones, Shunsaku Horiuchi, Tyco Mera et al.

Abstract

Stripped-envelope supernovae provide a window into how massive stars lose their layers, mix radioactive material, produce dust, and explode asymmetrically. We present optical, near-infrared (NIR), and mid-infrared (MIR) observations of SN~2025aico, a Type~IIb supernova in LEDA~35384.
