1.5D investigation of the Hanle effect in the Ca I 4227 line using partial frequency redistribution. Comparison of synthetic Stokes profiles in Bifrost and MURaM-ChE rMHD models

Devang Agnihotri, L. S. Anusha, D. Przybylski, R. H. Cameron, S. K. Solanki

Abstract

Modeling scattering polarization in the Ca I 4227 Å line is important for diagnosing chromospheric magnetic fields. We investigate the relative influence of formation heights and magnetic fields, through the Hanle effect, on spatially averaged synthetic Stokes profiles using realistic solar atmospheres.
