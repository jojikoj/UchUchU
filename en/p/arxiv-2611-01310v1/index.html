Forecasting the Ambient Solar Wind With a Physics-Informed Learned Inner Boundary: Part I-Differentiable Inverse Reconstruction From Solar Images

Enrico Camporeale, Andong Hu, Prateek Mayank, Thomas E. Berger

Abstract

The solar-wind speed at the inner boundary of heliospheric models is not observed; near-Earth monitors record the wind only after nonlinear propagation. We pose the reconstruction of an effective boundary speed at $21.5\,R_\odot$ as an inverse problem and train a map from solar observations to that boundary by differentiating through a reduced heliospheric transport solver from 21.5$\,R_\odot$ to measurement locations near the L1 Lagrangian point.
