G. Speranza, M. Pereira-Santaella, I. García-Bernete, E. González-Alfonso, S. Arribas, I. Lamperti, M. Perna, A. Alonso-Herrero et al.

We investigate the spatially resolved extinction and ice column densities in four nearby interacting (U)LIRGs using JWST/NIRSpec integral field spectroscopy (R$\sim$2700; 0.97--5.14 $μ$m).
